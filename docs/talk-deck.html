What it is

Like Wispr Flow — but local, and it adapts to you

Screamer runs entirely on your laptop on on-device models. It adapts to how you speak and how you write.

Hold left-control → it listens, transcribes, and styles your text. Instantly. Nothing leaves your machine.

The styling layer

It writes like you — not like AI

  • An LLM rewrites your raw transcript into clean writing in your own voice — stripping AI "slop" (em-dashes, buzzwords, filler).
  • We fine-tuned an NVIDIA open model to do it, scored against a slop metric (Kobak et al.) — it beats a frontier model's out-of-the-box output, significantly.
  • And it learns from you: every time you keep or revert a rewrite, that's the signal that personalizes it — on-device, from a handful of corrections.

Why it matters

Raise the floor — not just the ceiling

Most of AI is a race to raise the ceiling. Specialized, open intelligence can raise the floor — making AI work for people with disabilities, non-native speakers, anyone the frontier leaves behind.

That's the beauty of open source: intelligence anyone can own and adapt. Accessibility for all.
